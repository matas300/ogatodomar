The Place

An Eco Retreat in the Heart of Nature

Welcome to our Eco House in Viana do Castelo — a peaceful wooden retreat nestled in nature, just a short walk from the white sandy beach. Fall asleep to the calming ocean sound and wake up surrounded by trees and birdsong.

Whether you're stopping by during your Camino de Santiago journey or planning a cozy and romantic weekend, this is the perfect spot to unwind. The house is fully equipped with everything you need.

🌊 2 km from beach
🌲 Eco wooden house
🚴 Bikes available
